# TrueTerms design

An accessible, quiet document checking tool. White and warm paper surfaces, dark ink, and a deliberately chosen deep teal accent (#14635b) for actions and selections. No gradients, emojis, or decorative icons.

Body and control text starts at 18px. Georgia headings give a readable document character; Verdana supports plain, legible controls. Layout starts with one column and expands language choices into two columns where space permits. Content stays within a 48rem maximum width. Controls have at least 44px tap targets, visible keyboard focus, and clear labels.

Home, Upload, and Results share a wordmark and the exact required footer. The initial results table is intentionally empty.
